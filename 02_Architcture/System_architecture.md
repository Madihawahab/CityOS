# System Architecture

# CityOS

## AI-Powered Civic Operating System

Version 1.0

---

# 1. Overview

CityOS is designed as an AI-first, modular, cloud-native civic operating system that autonomously manages the complete lifecycle of civic issues.

The system consists of three independent portals powered by a centralized AI Intelligence Layer.

```
Citizen Portal
        │
        ▼
AI Intelligence Layer (Gemini + CityOS AI)
        │
        ▼
Authority Portal
        │
        ▼
City Intelligence Portal
```

All AI decision-making happens automatically inside the AI layer.

Authorities cannot override AI decisions. They execute the work assigned by the system.

---

# 2. System Components

## Citizen Portal

Purpose

Simple reporting interface for citizens.

Responsibilities

* Report civic issues
* Upload photos
* Upload videos
* Voice reporting
* Automatic GPS capture
* Anonymous reporting
* Track issue progress
* Verify completed repairs
* Receive notifications
* View nearby issues

---

## Authority Portal

Purpose

Operational workspace for municipal departments.

Responsibilities

* Receive AI-generated work assignments
* View issue details
* Upload repair evidence
* Update repair progress
* Complete assigned work
* View department performance

Authorities execute tasks generated by the AI system.

They cannot modify AI classification, priority, trust score, or department assignment.

---

## City Intelligence Portal

Purpose

Provide administrators with city-wide operational intelligence.

Responsibilities

* City analytics
* Infrastructure health
* Department KPIs
* Heatmaps
* Predictive maintenance
* AI transparency
* Audit logs
* Civic trends
* System monitoring

---

# 3. Central AI Intelligence Layer

The AI Intelligence Layer operates independently of all portals.

It continuously analyzes every issue submitted to the platform.

The AI Layer is composed of six autonomous intelligence modules.

---

## Module 1 — Report Intelligence

Input

* Images
* Videos
* Voice
* Text
* GPS

Tasks

* Detect issue type
* Generate description
* Estimate severity
* Extract metadata

Outputs

* Issue Category
* Confidence Score
* Severity

---

## Module 2 — Trust Engine

Tasks

* Detect duplicates
* Detect spam
* Verify authenticity
* Evaluate community support

Outputs

* Trust Score
* Duplicate Status
* Verification Status

---

## Module 3 — Decision Intelligence

Tasks

* Determine responsible department
* Calculate priority
* Estimate repair effort
* Predict completion time

Outputs

* Department Assignment
* Priority Level
* Estimated Resolution Time

---

## Module 4 — Resolution Intelligence

Tasks

* Compare before and after images
* Validate repair evidence
* Verify GPS
* Request citizen confirmation

Outputs

* Repair Verification
* Closure Recommendation

---

## Module 5 — Civic Intelligence

Tasks

* Generate heatmaps
* Detect trends
* Predict future failures
* Infrastructure analysis

Outputs

* Analytics
* Predictions
* Infrastructure Health

---

## Module 6 — Civic Copilot

Tasks

* Answer citizen questions
* Help authorities
* Summarize reports
* Generate insights

Outputs

* AI Responses
* Report Summaries
* Recommendations

# 7. Scalability

The architecture is modular.

Each AI module can evolve independently without affecting the rest of the system.

Additional departments, AI models, IoT devices, and smart city services can be integrated without redesigning the platform.
