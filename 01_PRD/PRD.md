# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

# CityOS

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Design Complete | Development Started

---

# 1. Vision

CityOS is an AI-powered Civic Operating System that autonomously manages the complete lifecycle of civic issues.

Instead of functioning as another complaint-reporting application, CityOS acts as an intelligent operating system connecting citizens, authorities, AI, and city administration into one seamless workflow.

Citizens report an issue once.

The AI understands it.

The AI verifies it.

The AI prioritizes it.

The AI routes it.

Authorities execute the assigned work.

Citizens verify the outcome.

The system continuously learns to improve future decisions.

# 4. Problem Statement

Citizens frequently encounter civic issues such as potholes, water leaks, garbage accumulation, broken streetlights, illegal dumping, and damaged public infrastructure.

Existing complaint systems suffer from several limitations:

* Manual categorization
* Duplicate complaints
* Poor routing
* Lack of transparency
* No verification of completed work
* Minimal citizen trust
* Fragmented workflows across departments

These inefficiencies result in delayed issue resolution and reduced public confidence.

CityOS addresses these challenges by introducing autonomous AI-driven decision making across the entire civic issue lifecycle.
